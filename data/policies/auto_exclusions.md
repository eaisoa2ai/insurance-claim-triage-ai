# Auto Policy Shared Exclusions

**Document ID:** AUTO-EXCL-001
**Applies To:** AUTO-STD-001, AUTO-COMP-001
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-01
**Currency:** EUR

This document lists exclusions that apply to all auto insurance policies issued under these terms. Where a policy document and this exclusions document conflict, this document takes precedence.

---

## Clause E.1 — Intentional Damage

Any damage deliberately caused by the policyholder, a named driver, or a person acting with the policyholder's knowledge or consent is excluded. This includes:

- Staged collisions or manufactured incidents
- Self-inflicted fire, flood, or mechanical damage
- Deliberate vandalism of the insured vehicle by the insured

A claim found to involve intentional damage will be rejected and reported to the relevant fraud authority.

---

## Clause E.2 — Unlicensed or Disqualified Driver

Damage sustained while the vehicle was being driven by a person without a valid driving licence, or by a person who has been disqualified from driving, is excluded from all coverage under all policies.

---

## Clause E.3 — Commercial and Ride-Share Use

Damage sustained while the insured vehicle is being used for any of the following purposes is excluded:

- Paid passenger transport (including ride-hailing services)
- Courier or delivery services
- Hire or rental to a third party
- Business use not declared at policy inception

If commercial use is discovered during a claim investigation, the claim will be rejected.

---

## Clause E.4 — Racing and Track Events

Damage sustained while the vehicle is participating in or practising for a race, rally, speed test, track day, or any off-road event is excluded from all coverage.

---

## Clause E.5 — Pre-Existing Damage

Damage that existed on the vehicle before the policy start date or before the reported incident is excluded. The insurer may use workshop records, previous claim history, and photographic evidence to identify pre-existing damage.

If a claim includes both new damage and pre-existing damage, only the new damage is eligible for settlement.

---

## Clause E.6 — Inconsistent Claimed Cause

A claim where the stated cause of damage is materially inconsistent with the physical evidence — including the damage photograph and the repair estimate — is not eligible for automatic settlement. Such claims are referred to manual review.

Examples:
- A customer states the vehicle was rear-ended in a collision, but the damage photograph shows burn damage only
- A customer claims front-end structural damage, but only a shattered rear window is visible in the submitted photograph
- A customer describes a minor parking scrape, but the photograph shows panel deformation consistent with a high-speed impact

If the inconsistency cannot be resolved by the adjuster, the claim is rejected.

---

## Clause E.7 — Late Reporting Beyond 90 Days

Claims reported more than **90 days** after the date of the incident are not eligible for settlement under any policy. This limit is absolute and cannot be waived.

Claims reported between 31 and 90 days after the incident are subject to the late reporting rules in the applicable policy document (AUTO-STD-001 Clause 4 or AUTO-COMP-001 Clause 4).

---

## Clause E.8 — Unverified Damage Without Evidence

Claims where no photograph of the damage is submitted, and where the repair estimate cannot be independently verified, are not eligible for automatic settlement. They are referred to manual review.

The adjuster may reject the claim if insufficient evidence exists to confirm that the reported damage occurred.

---

## Clause E.9 — Damage Outside Policy Coverage Type

Damage of a type explicitly excluded by the active policy is not covered, even if the policyholder believes it should be.

Examples:
- Fire damage under AUTO-STD-001 (collision-only policy): excluded
- Vandalism under AUTO-STD-001: excluded
- Glass-only damage under AUTO-STD-001: excluded

The policyholder is responsible for selecting a policy that covers the risks relevant to their situation.

---

## Clause E.10 — Misrepresentation

Any claim based on a materially false statement — including a false incident date, false location, false cause, or false description of the damage — will be rejected. The insurer reserves the right to recover costs already paid and to cancel the policy.
