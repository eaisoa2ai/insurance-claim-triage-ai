# Auto Basic Policy — AUTO-STD-001

**Policy Name:** Standard Auto Collision Cover
**Policy ID:** AUTO-STD-001
**Coverage Type:** Collision only
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-01
**Expiry Date:** 2026-12-31
**Currency:** EUR

---

## Clause 1 — What Is Covered

**Clause 1.1 — Collision Damage**
This policy covers physical damage to the insured vehicle caused by a collision with another vehicle, a stationary object, or a road surface, provided the insured was driving or the vehicle was lawfully parked at the time of the incident.

Covered damage types include:
- Dents, scrapes, and paint transfer resulting from a collision
- Crumpled panels or structural deformation caused by impact
- Broken taillights or headlights as a direct result of a collision
- Rear, front, or side panel damage from a collision event

**Clause 1.2 — Parking Lot and Hit-and-Run Incidents**
Damage sustained while the vehicle was lawfully parked is covered if the claimant provides a credible description of the incident. A damage photograph is required. If no photograph is available, the claim is referred to manual review (see Clause 5.2).

**Clause 1.3 — Single-Vehicle Collision**
Damage caused by the insured vehicle striking a fixed object — including walls, bollards, barriers, or kerbs — is covered under this policy, subject to the deductible in Clause 3.1.

---

## Clause 2 — What Is Not Covered

**Clause 2.1 — Fire and Burn Damage**
This policy does not cover any damage caused by fire, heat, or combustion, whether accidental or deliberate. Fire damage requires a comprehensive policy.

**Clause 2.2 — Flood and Water Damage**
Damage caused by flooding, submersion, or water ingress is not covered under this policy.

**Clause 2.3 — Theft and Vandalism**
Theft of the vehicle, theft of contents, and deliberate damage by a third party are not covered. These risks require a comprehensive policy.

**Clause 2.4 — Glass-Only Damage**
Damage limited to windscreens, windows, or sunroofs where no other vehicle damage is present is not covered under this policy. See the Auto Premium Policy (AUTO-COMP-001) for glass cover.

**Clause 2.5 — Storm and Falling Debris**
Damage caused by hail, storms, falling trees, or other weather events is not covered under this policy.

**Clause 2.6 — Pre-Existing Damage**
Damage that was present on the vehicle before the policy start date, or before the reported incident, is excluded from all claims under this policy.

**Clause 2.7 — Inconsistent Evidence**
Claims where the visible evidence — including damage photographs — is inconsistent with the reported cause of damage are not eligible for automatic settlement. Such claims are referred to manual review (see Clause 5.1).

For full exclusion details, see also: **auto_exclusions.md**.

## Clause 3 — Deductibles and Payout Limits

**Clause 3.1 — Standard Deductible**
A deductible of **€500** applies to every claim under this policy. The payout is calculated as:

> Payout = (Repair Estimate × Coverage Ratio) − €500

**Clause 3.2 — Coverage Ratio**
This policy covers **80%** of the verified repair cost after deductible. The insured is responsible for the remaining 20%.

**Clause 3.3 — Maximum Payout**
The maximum single-claim payout under this policy is **€15,000**. Repair estimates that exceed this limit are settled at the maximum payout amount, subject to manual approval (see Clause 5.3).

**Clause 3.4 — Minimum Claim Threshold**
Claims where the repair estimate is at or below the deductible (€500) result in a zero payout. The deductible is not refunded.

---

## Clause 4 — Late Reporting Rules

**Clause 4.1 — Reporting Deadline**
The insured must report the incident to the insurer within **30 days** of the date on which the damage occurred.

**Clause 4.2 — Late Reporting Escalation**
Claims reported between 31 and 60 days after the incident date are accepted but are automatically referred to manual review. A written explanation of the delay must be included in the claim submission.

**Clause 4.3 — Claims Rejected After 90 Days**
Claims reported more than 90 days after the incident date are not eligible for settlement under this policy. See also **auto_exclusions.md**, Clause E.7.

**Clause 4.4 — Date Verification**
The insurer reserves the right to verify the reported incident date against third-party sources including police reports, workshop records, and traffic camera data. Providing a false incident date is grounds for claim rejection.

---

## Clause 5 — Manual Approval Requirements

**Clause 5.1 — Inconsistent Damage Evidence**
A claim is referred to a senior adjuster for manual review when the damage photograph does not support the reported cause of damage — for example, when burn damage is visible but a collision is reported.

**Clause 5.2 — Missing Damage Photograph**
A claim submitted without a damage photograph is automatically referred to manual review. The adjuster will determine whether the claim can be settled based on the repair estimate and customer statement alone.

**Clause 5.3 — High-Value Claims**
Claims where the calculated payout exceeds **€5,000** require manual approval by a senior adjuster before settlement is issued.

**Clause 5.4 — High Claim Frequency**
Policyholders with three or more claims in any 24-month period are flagged for manual review on all subsequent claims within that period.

**Clause 5.5 — Late-Reported Claims**
Claims reported between 31 and 60 days after the incident (see Clause 4.2) are always referred to manual review, regardless of claim value.

---

## Clause 6 — Evidence Requirements

**Clause 6.1 — Mandatory Evidence**
The following evidence must be submitted with every claim:
- A photograph of the damage (minimum one image, clear and in focus)
- A written customer statement describing the incident
- The date and location of the incident

**Clause 6.2 — Supporting Evidence**
The following supporting evidence is strongly recommended and may be requested by the adjuster:
- Third-party contact details (where another vehicle was involved)
- Police report or crime reference number (for hit-and-run or vandalism)
- Witness statements

**Clause 6.3 — Evidence Adequacy**
If the submitted photograph does not clearly show the reported damage — for example, due to angle, distance, or image quality — the adjuster may request additional photographs before proceeding to settlement.

**Clause 6.4 — Repair Estimate**
A written repair estimate from a registered body shop must be submitted. The estimate must itemise parts and labour separately. Estimates that list parts not visible in the submitted photograph may be queried by the adjuster.

---

## Clause 7 — How Claims Are Settled

**Clause 7.1 — Automatic Settlement**
Claims that meet all of the following conditions are settled automatically without adjuster review:
- Damage photograph submitted and consistent with reported incident
- Repair estimate submitted and itemised
- Claim reported within 30 days of the incident
- Calculated payout does not exceed €5,000
- Policyholder has fewer than three claims in the past 24 months

**Clause 7.2 — Manual Settlement**
Claims that do not meet all conditions in Clause 7.1 are referred to a senior adjuster. The adjuster reviews the evidence, may request additional information, and issues an Approve or Reject decision with a written note.

**Clause 7.3 — Payout Calculation Example**
Repair estimate: €1,800 | Coverage ratio: 80% | Deductible: €500
→ Payout = (€1,800 × 0.80) − €500 = €1,440 − €500 = **€940**
