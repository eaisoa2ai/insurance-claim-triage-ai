# Auto Premium Policy — AUTO-COMP-001

**Policy Name:** Comprehensive Auto Cover
**Policy ID:** AUTO-COMP-001
**Coverage Type:** Collision + Comprehensive (fire, glass, vandalism, storm, flood)
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-01
**Expiry Date:** 2026-12-31
**Currency:** EUR

---

## Clause 1 — What Is Covered

**Clause 1.1 — Collision Damage**
All collision damage covered under AUTO-STD-001 (Clause 1.1, 1.2, 1.3) is included in this policy. The terms and conditions for collision claims are identical to the Standard Auto Collision Policy unless explicitly superseded below.

**Clause 1.2 — Glass Damage**
This policy covers the full cost of replacing or repairing a cracked, chipped, or shattered windscreen, rear window, side window, or sunroof. Both the front windscreen and all other glass are covered.

Glass coverage applies regardless of whether other vehicle damage is present.

**Clause 1.3 — Vandalism and Deliberate Damage by a Third Party**
Damage caused deliberately by a third party — including smashed windows, key scratches, broken mirrors, and tyre slashing — is covered under this policy. A police report or crime reference number must be submitted with the claim (see Clause 6.2).

**Clause 1.4 — Fire and Burn Damage**
Accidental fire damage to the insured vehicle is covered, including engine fires, electrical fires, and fire spreading from an adjacent vehicle. Deliberately set fires are excluded (see Clause 2.1).

**Clause 1.5 — Storm and Falling Debris**
Damage caused by hailstorms, high winds, falling trees, or masonry is covered under this policy. The claimant must provide a photograph clearly showing the damage and, where possible, evidence of the weather event (e.g. a date from a news source).

**Clause 1.6 — Flood and Water Ingress**
Damage caused by flooding or sustained water ingress is covered, subject to the deductible in Clause 3.1.

---

## Clause 2 — What Is Not Covered

**Clause 2.1 — Intentional Damage by the Insured**
Any damage deliberately caused by the policyholder or a person acting on their behalf is excluded. This includes deliberate fires, staged collisions, and self-inflicted vandalism.

**Clause 2.2 — Incorrect Cause Reported**
If the stated cause of damage is found to be inconsistent with the visible evidence — for example, claiming storm debris damage when the image shows burn damage — the claim is not eligible for automatic settlement and is referred to manual review (see Clause 5.1).

**Clause 2.3 — Pre-Existing Damage**
Damage that was present on the vehicle before the policy start date, or before the reported incident, is excluded from all claims under this policy.

**Clause 2.4 — Commercial and Ride-Share Use**
Damage sustained while the vehicle is being used for commercial purposes, ride-hailing, or courier deliveries is not covered under this policy.

For full exclusion details, see also: **auto_exclusions.md**.

## Clause 3 — Deductibles and Payout Limits

**Clause 3.1 — Standard Deductible**
A deductible of **€250** applies to all non-glass claims under this policy. The payout is calculated as:

> Payout = (Repair Estimate × Coverage Ratio) − €250

**Clause 3.2 — Glass Deductible**
Glass-only claims (Clause 1.2) carry a **€0 deductible**. The full verified cost of glass repair or replacement is covered, up to the glass sub-limit in Clause 3.5.

**Clause 3.3 — Coverage Ratios**

| Damage Type | Coverage Ratio |
|---|---|
| Collision | 80% |
| Glass | 100% |
| Vandalism | 100% |
| Fire | 90% |
| Storm / Debris | 90% |
| Flood | 80% |

**Clause 3.4 — Maximum Payout**
The maximum single-claim payout under this policy is **€25,000**.

**Clause 3.5 — Glass Sub-Limit**
Glass-only claims are capped at **€1,500** per claim. Damage exceeding this amount requires manual approval (see Clause 5.3).

**Clause 3.6 — Minimum Claim Threshold**
Claims where the repair estimate is at or below the deductible result in a zero payout. The deductible is not refunded.

---

## Clause 4 — Late Reporting Rules

**Clause 4.1 — Reporting Deadline**
The insured must report the incident within **45 days** of the date on which the damage occurred.

**Clause 4.2 — Late Reporting with Valid Reason**
Claims reported between 46 and 90 days after the incident are accepted if the claimant provides documentary evidence of a reason for the delay — such as a hospital discharge letter, a police report showing investigation was ongoing, or written confirmation from a third-party insurer. Late claims with valid supporting documentation are referred to manual review.

**Clause 4.3 — Claims Rejected After 90 Days**
Claims reported more than 90 days after the incident date are not eligible for settlement under this policy. See also **auto_exclusions.md**, Clause E.7.

**Clause 4.4 — Glass Claims — Relaxed Reporting**
Glass-only claims (Clause 1.2) where the damage is confirmed by a registered glazing workshop receipt dated within the reporting window are accepted up to 60 days after the incident without referral to manual review.

---

## Clause 5 — Manual Approval Requirements

**Clause 5.1 — Inconsistent Damage Evidence**
A claim is referred to a senior adjuster for manual review when the damage photograph or repair estimate does not support the reported cause of damage.

**Clause 5.2 — Missing Damage Photograph**
Claims submitted without a photograph are automatically referred to manual review, with the exception of glass claims under €500 submitted with a valid repair receipt from a registered workshop.

**Clause 5.3 — High-Value Claims**
Claims where the calculated payout exceeds **€10,000** require manual approval by a senior adjuster before settlement is issued.

**Clause 5.4 — High Claim Frequency**
Policyholders with three or more claims in any 24-month period are flagged for manual review on all subsequent claims within that period.

**Clause 5.5 — Fire or Total Loss**
All claims involving fire damage or a total-loss assessment are referred to manual review regardless of claim value.

---

## Clause 6 — Evidence Requirements

**Clause 6.1 — Mandatory Evidence**
The following evidence must be submitted with every claim:
- A photograph of the damage
- A written customer statement
- The date and location of the incident

**Clause 6.2 — Vandalism and Break-In Claims**
A police report number or crime reference number must be submitted for all vandalism and theft claims within 7 days of the claim submission. Claims without this reference are held pending and referred to manual review after 7 days.

**Clause 6.3 — Fire Claims**
Fire claims must include a photograph clearly showing burn damage. A fire brigade attendance record or insurance investigator report may be requested for claims above €3,000.

**Clause 6.4 — Storm and Debris Claims**
Storm damage claims must include a photograph showing the debris or impact point. The customer statement must describe the weather conditions. The insurer may cross-reference the incident date against publicly available weather records.

**Clause 6.5 — Glass Claims — Relaxed Evidence**
Glass claims below €500 may be settled on the basis of a dated repair receipt from a registered workshop, without a damage photograph, subject to adjuster discretion.

**Clause 6.6 — Repair Estimate**
A written repair estimate from a registered body shop must be submitted for all non-glass claims. The estimate must itemise parts and labour separately.

---

## Clause 7 — How Claims Are Settled

**Clause 7.1 — Automatic Settlement**
Claims that meet all of the following conditions are settled automatically:
- Damage photograph submitted and consistent with the reported incident
- Repair estimate submitted and itemised (or workshop receipt for glass)
- Claim reported within the deadline in Clause 4.1
- Calculated payout does not exceed €10,000
- Policyholder has fewer than three claims in the past 24 months
- No fire damage or total-loss assessment

**Clause 7.2 — Manual Settlement**
Claims referred to manual review under any clause in Section 5 are reviewed by a senior adjuster, who issues an Approve or Reject decision with a written note recorded in the audit trail.

**Clause 7.3 — Payout Calculation Examples**

*Vandalism (broken window):*
Repair cost: €680 | Coverage ratio: 100% | Deductible: €0 (glass)
→ Payout = €680 × 1.00 − €0 = **€680**

*Collision:*
Repair estimate: €2,500 | Coverage ratio: 80% | Deductible: €250
→ Payout = (€2,500 × 0.80) − €250 = €2,000 − €250 = **€1,750**

*Fire damage:*
Repair estimate: €4,000 | Coverage ratio: 90% | Deductible: €250
→ Payout = (€4,000 × 0.90) − €250 = €3,600 − €250 = **€3,350**
